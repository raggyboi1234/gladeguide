    What do the labels mean?

    These species are protected under the U.S. Endangered Species Act, which sorts them into two groups. It is illegal to harm, harass, or capture them without a permit.

    Endangered
    The species is in danger of extinction throughout all or a significant part of its range.
    Threatened
    The species is likely to become endangered in the foreseeable future if nothing changes.

    Some species have moved from endangered to threatened as protections and habitat work helped their numbers recover. Threatened does not mean safe: these animals still depend on the wetlands and clean water of South Florida.
